# Accounts

The Accounts page is the inventory of everything JumpServer manages for privileged access management: the accounts themselves, the assets they belong to, and reusable account templates. It opens on the **Accounts** tab by default.

## Overview

Go to **PAM > Accounts**. The page contains three tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Accounts** | Every account managed by PAM, with its asset, platform, secret type, and connectivity state. |
| **Assets** | The managed assets together with their accounts, last login, and connectivity. |
| **Account templates** | Reusable definitions of username, secret type, and privileged flag used to create accounts in bulk. |

All three tabs are organization-scoped, and the buttons you see depend on your account permissions. A user who cannot view accounts sees an empty or restricted list rather than the full inventory.

## Accounts tab

The **Accounts** tab lists managed accounts with the columns **Name**, **Username**, **Secret**, **Asset**, **Platform**, **Connect**, and **Actions**. The **Secret** column never shows the value; use the row actions to view or update it if your role allows.

![Figure 1: Account list with quick filters and row actions](/images/v4/jumpserver/V4_pam_accounts1.png)

Use the quick filters above the table to narrow the list by the dimension that matters for review:

| Filter group | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Recent (7 days)** | Recently discovered, Recently logged in, Recently modified, Recently changed password, Recent password change failed |
| **Risky account** | No login for long time, Add account, Weak password, Empty password, Long time password |
| **Account type** | All, Host, Device, Database, Cloud, Web, Directories, Other |

Typical actions from this tab:

1. Click **Create** to add one account manually and link it to an asset.
2. Click **Add from template** to create accounts on one or more assets from a saved template.
3. Open the **More** menu on a row to edit the account, update or clear its secret, verify it, duplicate it, or delete it.

<Alert type="note">
Account creation is also available from the asset detail page in the Console. The PAM page is a cross-asset view, so it is usually faster when you need to work with accounts from several assets at once.
</Alert>

## Assets tab

The **Assets** tab inverts the relationship and lists the managed assets with their accounts. Columns are **Name**, **Address**, **Accounts**, **Platform**, **Last login date**, **Connectivity**, and **Actions**.

The **Connectivity** column reports the connection state for the asset accounts, for example **OK**, **Error**, or **Invalid password error**. A failed state usually means the stored secret no longer matches the remote account, so update the secret before relying on automated tasks.

## Account templates tab

The **Account templates** tab lists saved templates with **Name**, **Username**, **Secret type**, **Privileged**, and **Actions**.

A template fixes the username pattern, secret type, and privileged flag so that operators do not have to repeat those choices. After a template exists, select it from **Add from template** on the **Accounts** tab: choose the target assets, confirm the accounts to create, and submit. This is the recommended way to roll out a standard administrative account across a large asset group.

Click **Create** on the **Accounts** or **Account templates** tab to add a single account or template; the **Assets** tab has its own **Create** action for new assets. For asset-level settings that are not account-specific, see [Assets](/features/console/assets/).
