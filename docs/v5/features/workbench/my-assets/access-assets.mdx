title: "Access Assets"
description: Browse authorized assets in the Workbench, inspect allowed accounts, save favorites, and start a connection.
---

**Access Assets** lists the assets your account has been authorized to use. From
this page you can filter by node, search, inspect the accounts you may log in
with, mark assets as favorites, and open a connection. Choosing the protocol,
account, and connection method is completed in the web terminal.

![Figure 1: The Access Assets page](/images/jumpserver/v5_user_wb_assets_01.png)

## Prerequisites

* You are signed in and can open the Workbench. Regular users land there by
  default; administrators switch the workspace first.
* An administrator has authorized the assets for your account. See
  [Authorizations](/features/console/policies/authorizations).
* The correct organization is selected in the organization switcher. Assets are
  authorized per organization.

<Alert type="note">
If the list is empty, confirm that the assets are authorized for your account and
that you are in the organization where the authorization was created. Changing
the organization reloads the list.
</Alert>

## Browse and filter

The page is split into a tree on the left and an asset list on the right.

* Use the **Authorized tree** on the left to filter by node and category. The
  numbers next to nodes indicate how many assets they contain.
* Use the search box in the top-right corner to find an asset by name or address.
* Use the column settings icon to show or hide optional columns, and the refresh
  icon to reload permissions.
* Click a node in the tree to scope the table to that node. Click the same node
  again, or the parent node, to widen the list.

## Inspect accounts and favorites

* The **Accounts** column shows a **View** link. Click it to open the list of
  accounts your account may use on that asset, including the account type and
  connectivity status.
* Click the star in the **Actions** column to add or remove an asset from your
  favorites. Favorites are shown in the web terminal's favorites list, so they
  are quick to reach during a connection.
* Select multiple rows and use **More actions** to add or remove favorites in
  bulk.
* Asset names and comments can be renamed inline for your own view. These
  personal aliases and comments do not rename the asset for other users.

## Start a connection

<Steps>

<div>
Find the target asset with the tree, the search box, or the favorites you saved.
</div>

<div>
Click the connect icon in the **Actions** column. The connect action is only
offered for active assets.
</div>

<div>
The web terminal opens in a new window with the asset preselected. Choose the
protocol and account there and connect.
</div>

</Steps>

The connection itself, including protocol selection, account selection, and
connection options, is handled by the [Web Terminal](/features/workbench/web-terminal/).

<Alert type="tip">
To connect from a local terminal instead of a browser, use
[SSH Terminal](/features/workbench/ssh-terminal).
</Alert>

## Column reference

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Asset name. Can be given a personal alias inline. |
| Address | Asset IP address or hostname |
| Platform | Asset platform, such as Linux or Windows |
| Accounts | Authorized accounts on the asset; click **View** to list them |
| Labels | Asset labels used for grouping and filtering |
| Connectivity | Whether the asset is currently reachable |
| Actions | Connect to the asset, or add it to favorites |

<Alert type="tip">
Favorites created here are personal to your account and appear in the web
terminal. Removing a favorite does not change the authorization, and it never
deletes the asset.
</Alert>
