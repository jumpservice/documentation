title: "Awaiting Approval"
description: Review tickets assigned to you as a handler, adjust the requested scope, and approve or reject requests.
---

<Alert type="xpack">
**Tickets & Approvals** requires JumpServer Enterprise Edition. Ticket entries, approval cards, and flow settings are hidden without a license.
</Alert>

**Assigned** lists the tickets that are waiting for your decision. A ticket appears
here when you are the current handler in its approval flow and the ticket is still
pending. Use it as the approver work queue: read the request, tighten the scope if
needed, then approve or reject. In the navigation, this page is labelled
**Assigned**; its page title is **Awaiting Approval**.

![Figure 1: A ticket awaiting approval](/images/jumpserver/workorder05.png)

## Prerequisites

* The Tickets feature is enabled and licensed. The **Tickets & Approvals** area
  requires an enterprise license.
* Your role grants the ticket permission needed to view and process approvals.
* You are named as a handler in the ticket's approval flow, or you belong to a
  group or role matched by an attribute rule. Being able to view tickets does not
  by itself put a ticket in this queue.

## Review and decide

<Steps>

<div>
Open **Assigned**. Only pending tickets where you are the current handler are
listed.
</div>

<div>
Click the ticket **title** to open its detail page.
</div>

<div>
Read **Basic info** and **Apply info**: applicant, requested assets or nodes,
accounts, actions, and the requested validity period.
</div>

<div>
Check the approval steps and the conversation on the right side. Add a **Reply** if
you need more information from the applicant.
</div>

<div>
If necessary, narrow the requested assets, accounts, or actions before deciding.
Approvers can reduce the requested scope so the permission matches the least
privilege needed.
</div>

<div>
Click **Agree** to approve the request or **Reject** to decline it. The ticket moves
to the next approval level, or finishes when the last level is complete.
</div>

</Steps>

![Figure 2: Approving a ticket and replying to the applicant](/images/jumpserver/workorder06.png)

## Batch approval

Select several pending tickets and choose **Batch approval** in the toolbar. A
confirmation dialog lists the selected tickets and their key request details.
Confirm to approve all of them, or cancel to reject the whole selection. Use batch
actions only when the requests have the same scope you would approve
individually.

## Ticket detail reference

| Area | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Applicant, type, ticket flow | Who submitted the request and which flow processes it |
| Status | Pending, approved, rejected, or completed |
| Assignees | All handlers for the current approval level |
| Current handler | The handler who is expected to act now |
| CC users | Users copied on the ticket; they receive updates but do not approve |
| Apply info | Requested nodes, assets, accounts, actions, and validity dates |
| Approval steps | Progress through the flow, including each level's result |
| Comments | Conversation between applicant, handlers, and CC users |

## Column reference

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| No. | Ticket serial number |
| Title | Ticket title; click to open the detail page |
| Type | Ticket type, such as Apply for assets |
| Status | Current ticket status |
| Date | Creation time of the ticket |

<Alert type="note">
Approvers bound to WeCom or DingTalk can also process tickets from the mobile
approval message without signing in to JumpServer. Decisions made there use the
same approval flow and are recorded in the ticket history.
</Alert>
