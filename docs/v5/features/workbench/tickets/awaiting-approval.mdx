# Related to Me

<Alert type="xpack">
  **Tickets & Approvals** requires JumpServer Enterprise Edition. Ticket
  entries, approval cards, and flow settings are hidden without a license.
</Alert>

**Related to me** groups the tickets you participate in. In the navigation, this
page is labelled **Related to me**.

<VersionBadge version="5.2.0" kind="new" />

| Tab             | What it shows                                                                      |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Assigned        | Pending tickets that are waiting for your decision as the current handler.         |
| Processed by me | Tickets you approved, rejected, or otherwise processed.                            |
| Copied to me    | Tickets where you are copied as a CC user; you receive updates but do not approve. |

![Figure 1: Related to me with Assigned, Processed by me, and Copied to me tabs.](/images/v5/jumpserver/v5_ticket_related_to_me_01.png)

## Prerequisites

- The Tickets feature is enabled and licensed.
- Your role grants the ticket permission needed to view and process approvals.
- For **Assigned**, you are the current handler in the ticket flow, or you belong
  to a group or role matched by its approver rule.
- For **Copied to me**, you are a CC user on the ticket.

## Review and decide

<Steps>

<div>
  Open **Related to me** and select **Assigned**. Only pending tickets where you
  are the current handler are listed.
</div>

<div>
  If needed, use the **Type** filter to show one ticket type or all types. See
  [Ticket types](/features/workbench/tickets/my-tickets#ticket-types) for the
  supported types.
</div>

<div>Click the ticket **title** to open its detail page.</div>

<div>
  Read **Basic info** and **Apply info**: applicant, requested assets or nodes,
  accounts, actions, and the requested validity period.
</div>

<div>
  Check the approval steps and the conversation on the right side. Add a
  **Reply** if you need more information from the applicant.
</div>

<div>
  If necessary, narrow the requested assets, accounts, or actions before
  deciding. Approvers can reduce the requested scope so the permission matches
  the least privilege needed.
</div>

<div>
  Click **Agree** to approve the request or **Reject** to decline it. The ticket
  moves to the next approval level, or finishes when the last level is complete.
</div>

</Steps>

![Figure 2: Approving a ticket and replying to the applicant](/images/v5/jumpserver/workorder06.png)

## Processed and copied tickets

- **Processed by me** shows the tickets you previously approved, rejected, or
  otherwise processed. Open a ticket to review the decision and its history; this
  tab does not provide new approval actions.
- **Copied to me** shows the tickets where you are a CC user. You receive updates
  and can follow the conversation, but you do not approve or reject the request.

## Batch approval

Open **Assigned** and select several pending tickets, then choose **Batch
approval** in the toolbar. A confirmation dialog lists the selected tickets and
their key request details. Confirm to approve all of them, or cancel to reject the
whole selection. Use batch actions only when the requests have the same scope you
would approve individually.

## Ticket detail reference

| Area                         | What it shows                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Applicant, type, ticket flow | Who submitted the request and which flow processes it               |
| Status                       | Pending, approved, rejected, or completed                           |
| Assignees                    | All handlers for the current approval level                         |
| Current handler              | The handler who is expected to act now                              |
| CC users                     | Users copied on the ticket; they receive updates but do not approve |
| Apply info                   | Requested nodes, assets, accounts, actions, and validity dates      |
| Approval steps               | Progress through the flow, including each level's result            |
| Comments                     | Conversation between applicant, handlers, and CC users              |

## Column reference

| Column          | Description                                       |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Title           | Ticket title; click to open the detail page       |
| No.             | Ticket serial number                              |
| Type            | Ticket type, such as Apply for assets             |
| Organization    | Organization that owns the ticket                 |
| Approval status | Current approval state, such as Awaiting approval |
| Date            | Creation time of the ticket                       |

<Alert type="note">
  Approvers bound to WeCom or DingTalk can also process tickets from the mobile
  approval message without signing in to JumpServer. Decisions made there use
  the same approval flow and are recorded in the ticket history.
</Alert>
