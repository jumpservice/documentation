# Organizations

<Alert type="xpack">
**Multi-organization management and organization isolation** require JumpServer Enterprise Edition.
</Alert>

JumpServer separates data by **organization**. Users, user groups, assets, accounts,
labels, and authorizations all belong to an organization. When you are a member of
more than one organization, use the organization selector to change the active
context.

## Switch organizations

1. In the top navigation, select the current organization name.
2. In **Change organization**, choose another organization.
3. The page reloads and every list, search, and permission check now uses the new
   organization.

The current organization is highlighted in the selector. Switching organizations
does not change your login session, password, or personal preferences, but it can
change which menus and actions are available because your role may differ between
organizations.

![Organization selector](/images/v5/jumpserver/organization_switch01.png)

## Manage organizations

Users with the required system permission can use **Manage orgs** and **Create
organization** from the same selector. Organization management is also available
under **System Settings > Organizations**. See [System Settings > Organizations](/features/system-settings/general/organizations)
for the administrative workflow.

## Missing organizations

Organizations are isolated. If an organization does not appear in the selector, you
have not been added to it. An administrator of that organization must invite you and
assign an organization role before you can switch to it. The special **Global**
organization is visible only to system administrators.

## Web terminal organization scope

The Web terminal (Luna) has its own organization selector in the left panel. The
**All organizations** option aggregates assets that are authorized to you across
organizations. Use it when you need one place to find an authorized asset, and use a
specific organization when you want to stay within one scope.

![Organization selector in the Web terminal](/images/v5/jumpserver/organization_switch02.png)

## Notes

- Perform create, update, and delete operations in the organization that owns the
  record.
- If menus change immediately after switching, that is expected: the active
  organization determines your effective role and permissions.
