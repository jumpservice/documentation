# Community vs Enterprise

JumpServer Community Edition is free and self-hosted for up to 5,000 assets.
Enterprise Edition adds custom RBAC, multi-tenant organizations, enterprise SSO,
advanced database and connection methods, and SLA-backed support. The table below
compares the two editions feature by feature.
